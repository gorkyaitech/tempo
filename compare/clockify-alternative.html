Looking for a Clockify alternative after the free-plan changes? (2026)

Clockify built its name on the most generous free plan in time tracking. In April 2026 that changed: CAKE.com announced free-plan restrictions — including moving billable rates and CSV/Excel export to paid tiers. Here's an honest look at what that means for solo users.

What changed at Clockify

Per the official announcement and current pricing page: the free tier is now capped (5 users, 1-month report range shown on the pricing page), and features long taken for granted — billable rates, CSV/Excel export, shared report links — require a paid plan ($3.99–$11.99/user/mo). Clockify remains a very capable product, especially for teams that grow into its paid tiers.

The sticking point for many solo users is principle, not price: exporting your own recorded hours is now a paid feature.

Stay with Clockify if…

…you're a team, you rely on its integrations or kiosk/scheduling features, or your workflow lives in its reports. Its paid tiers are competitively priced for teams, and the product is mature.

Switch to Tempo if…

…you're one person tracking your own work, you believe exporting your own hours should never cost money, and you'd rather have an app that catches forgotten time than a team dashboard. Tempo has no account, works offline, and its free tier is the whole tracking product — not a trial.

Structural, not promotional

Tempo's free plan can't be walked back the same way.

Your hours live on your device, exports are local, and there's no login between you and your data. Try it with a sample week — nothing to sign up for.

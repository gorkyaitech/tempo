How billable hours are calculated

Billable amount = hours worked × hourly rate. The catch is the hours: studies of freelancers consistently find 10–20% of billable income is lost to untracked work — the quick call, the revision round, the afternoon nobody logged. Calculating is the easy half; capturing is where the money leaks.

Decimal vs clock format

This calculator accepts both: 1.75 and 1:45 are the same hour and three-quarters. Invoices usually show decimal hours; timesheets often use h:mm.

Common rounding rules

Many professionals bill in 6-minute (0.1h) or 15-minute (0.25h) increments, always rounding up to the next increment per task. Whatever rule you use, state it on the invoice and apply it consistently.

How do I bill different rates for one client?

Add one line per rate — e.g., "Acme — design" at $95 and "Acme — meetings" at $60. The total combines them.
